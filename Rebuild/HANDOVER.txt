Why it may be slow (code evidence, not a measured diagnosis)
----------------------------------------------------------
Park All serializes windows; each workflow serializes tabs. Observer calls read
all terminal windows, process lists and provider identities repeatedly. Gates,
archive authorization and post-operation verification can repeat full scans.
Process cwd/listener checks use subprocesses. Codex calls initialize local RPC
connections. Client shutdown has a bounded wait of up to 45 seconds per client;
operation completion and observer settling add their own bounded waits.
Measure actual stages and call counts before changing safety checks. Prefer
reusing a coherent observation within a bounded operation over removing guards
or adding unsafe broad parallel mutations. Native operations are not an atomic
transaction, so individual subsystem passes did not prove their combined timing.
